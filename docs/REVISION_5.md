# Integrated edition: version comparison and editorial assessment

20 October 2026. Literature snapshot: 19 October 2026.

This assessment describes the integrated base edition. Subsequent refinements add coverage, diagnostics, terminology, and generated catalog/evidence views; [BUILD.md](BUILD.md) describes the current manuscript and pagination.

The integrated edition is the recommended manuscript. It retains the twenty-round edition's technical formulation and ten vector figures, while developing a more continuous scholarly argument. The organizing idea is the learning signal preserved by numerical and learning interventions, together with the computation needed to preserve it.

## Substantive changes

The abstract, introduction, section bridges, synthesis, research agenda, and conclusion now develop the same scientific argument. Numerical design changes the computation that supplies experience; learning interventions change how that experience affects the next policy. The comparison turns on what is shared, what is preserved, and what remains unresolved.

RQ labels and four Synthesis callouts were removed. Question-form comparison cells became statements of dependencies and consequences. A redundant conditions-of-interpretation table was removed because its content was already developed in the evidence figure, the synthesis, and the limitations. Specific within-study observations, their source attribution, and the complete Surveyed Papers table remain.

The text distinguishes mechanisms without implying a universal ranking: conditional versus response-level correction, agreement versus higher-precision fidelity, estimator correction versus update regulation, and rollout speed versus training efficiency. Necessary assumptions remain next to the relevant equations or source claims. Appendix source selection describes the review method without recounting the collection's editing history. The README foregrounds the research findings and keeps editorial history outside the main presentation.

## Editorial assessment

The framework was already sound; its strongest improvement is integration. Earlier versions often presented a definition, a method list, and a caution as separate units. The integrated edition explains how the definition determines the mechanism, how a component acts on that mechanism, and what the published comparison establishes. This is more useful than adding further categories or figures.

The remaining limits are scientific: curated coverage, heterogeneous primary experiments, limited direct low-bit evidence for schedule adaptation, and no independent replication. Further progress depends chiefly on subject-matter review and stronger primary evidence. Repeated stylistic editing cannot remove those limits, and this assessment does not claim a flawless or externally validated manuscript.

The final edition has 28 pages, eight main sections, twenty focal studies, twenty-six references, and ten vector figures. All eleven displayed equations and the appendix's twenty paper entries are unchanged from the technical base. Compilation, citation and section mappings, PDF synchronization, and final visual checks are recorded in [BUILD.md](BUILD.md).
