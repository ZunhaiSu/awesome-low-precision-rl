# Current editorial assessment

The 22 October 2026 revision retains the original template, eight main sections and the 20 October literature snapshot. The central argument now runs from the GRPO learning loop to numerical failure mechanisms, component interventions, and evidence on quality and total cost.

The background supplies an end-to-end reward-to-update example and a compact explanation of W/A/KV, QAT and native arithmetic. Exact response-level likelihood details remain available in Appendix C. Four decision questions define the primary classes; the distinction between estimator correction and update control is operational and permits multi-component papers.

Earlier pivotal methods receive fuller treatment alongside TRACE, Score Centering and TRIAGE. The synthesis foregrounds shared computation, preservation of useful updates, and end-to-end learning efficiency. Direct-comparison graphs and source-specific result tables serve different roles. The companion README follows the same argument while retaining complete paper titles, links and the numbered reading library.

This is a focused narrative survey of 29 contributions. Its evidence remains heterogeneous and source-reported. The framework organizes and interprets that evidence; it does not establish an exhaustive corpus, independent reproduction, a universal ranking, or a new benchmark. The next substantive advance would come from new evidence on mechanisms and combinations rather than additional taxonomic subdivisions.
