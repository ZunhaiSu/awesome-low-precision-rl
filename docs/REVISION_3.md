# Five-pass revision: a more connected survey

Date: 2026-10-19. This revision preserves the eight-section scientific framework while strengthening the argument, source coverage, and prose. Five successive source and PDF snapshots were compared during editing; the manuscript compiled after each pass.

## Assessment

The framework is coherent: policy definitions establish what differs, mechanism analysis explains why it matters, numerical and learning interventions describe what changes, and synthesis connects those changes to published outcomes. The main weakness was presentation. Repeated instructions about how a reader should compare papers interrupted the scientific argument, and several method descriptions read like separate catalog entries. Evidence records also covered only six of the thirteen core studies.

The revised manuscript develops a more continuous account of the learning process. Its central contribution is the connection between policy semantics, intervention components, and evidence, rather than the number of papers collected. The result is a stronger scholarly draft for author and subject-matter review. Curated source selection, heterogeneous experiments, limited direct low-bit schedule evidence, and lack of independent replication remain substantive limits.

## Examples of the prose revision

**From a writing instruction to an explanation.**

Before: “These interventions should be compared through the decisions they alter. Does the rule retain a token that would otherwise be clipped, reject an entire response, or change the balance of positive and negative updates?”

After: “The shared issue is which learning signal reaches the parameter update. A token-level boundary can restore a contribution lost to clipping, whereas a sequence-level trust band controls a whole response.”

**From a reporting proposal to a research connection.**

Before: “The field would benefit from small, explicit evidence contracts that travel with a result.”

After: “Transfer becomes easier to assess when results identify the learner and sampler paths, actual behavior probabilities, update ratios, collection schedule, evaluation precision, and timing scope.”

**From repeated caution to synthesis.**

Before: “The findings jointly support a conditional conclusion: execution fidelity and learning correction are complementary resources, and the useful balance depends on which failure is active.”

After: “These findings connect fidelity and correction through the learning signal they preserve. Better execution can change which tokens require correction; a better update rule can make residual execution error tolerable.”

## Scope of the assessment

The five passes are editorial and technical self-review, not external peer review. No new experiments, performance rankings, exhaustive-search claims, or acceptance probabilities were added. Source observations retain their setting-specific limits. Current compilation, portability, and visual checks are recorded in [BUILD.md](BUILD.md).
